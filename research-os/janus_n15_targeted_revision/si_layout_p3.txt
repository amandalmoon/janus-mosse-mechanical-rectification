The edge-weight test is intentionally not an atomistic edge-relaxation model. Across all ten tested cut/weight combinations the A/B

barrier, continuous twist sweep, or hysteresis was calculated. The reduced-model claim is therefore competing-registry switching with
edge-sensitive equilibrium location, not a universal kinetic reversal twist.
