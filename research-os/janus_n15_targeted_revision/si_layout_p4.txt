S9. In-plane relaxation hierarchy
The rigid-model result was challenged first by allowing all N=127 sites to relax in plane under a free-edge triangular finite-element
membrane. The same published 2H GSFE is retained at each local registry. Internal x/y translations and infinitesimal rigid rotation are
projected out so that global registry and imposed twist remain external coordinates. The source Angeli GSFE already includes local
relaxation of the interlayer separation at each sampled aligned registry; the present extensions therefore test collective in-plane finite-
contact compliance, not the local vertical separation already folded into the GSFE.
