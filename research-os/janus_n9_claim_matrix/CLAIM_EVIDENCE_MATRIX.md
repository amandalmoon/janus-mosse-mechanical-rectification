## Freeze rules for the manuscript rewrite

1. A claim marked VERIFIED may be used only inside its listed scope.
2. A claim marked REJECTED must not reappear through legacy text, captions, SI, or figure annotations.
3. NOT_ESTABLISHED items belong in limitations/future work, not as positive conclusions.
4. Novelty status is separate from numerical validity. LIKELY novelty does not become VERIFIED without a final literature reverse search.
5. The new thermal distinction is mandatory: v-component boundary (0.60/0.65) and full-vector boundary (0.65/0.70) are different observables.
6. Guided k_perp=25 remains a secondary comparison, never the canonical headline baseline.
